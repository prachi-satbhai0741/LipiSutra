How the Decoder Works

Agent 1 - OCR and Transliteration

The uploaded artifact is passed to a vision model that detects the script and produces a literal transliteration of every clearly visible glyph.

Agent 2 - Gap Filling and Translation

Torn and faded regions are reconstructed from context, then the restored text is rendered into modern Marathi and summarised in English.

Platform Roles

Public, Historian and Museum Admin

A reader uploads and reads. A historian corrects and verifies. An administrator monitors the digitization statistics of the collection.

Did you know?

The Modi script was the administrative hand of the Maratha empire and is today read fluently by only a few hundred people.
